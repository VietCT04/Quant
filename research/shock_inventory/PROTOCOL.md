# Shock accumulation and recovery distribution

## Hypotheses and fixed design

The strategy tests whether temporary price pressure is large enough to overcome taker fees, spread and slippage in a market-order inventory policy. Acceleration, relative returns and aggressive trade flow represent different reasons that a dislocation might reverse. Causal inputs use completed five-second bars. One-minute return, previous one-minute return, current five-minute move, prior rolling volatility, 30-minute trend and lagged 20-minute EMA distinguish the size and speed of shocks from persistent direction. The short-side version is assessed independently. Research on liquidity provision motivates the mechanism in other markets. [Reversals and the Returns to Liquidity Provision](https://www.nber.org/papers/w30917)

- Round one uses the existing V2 defaults, a 30-second decision clock, and 10 USDT market orders at the next five-second open. The inventory limit is a strategy-local 200 USDT per symbol and there is no unconditional size escalation. Another add needs a new observed adverse extreme, an additional price step, and a still-active shock. The target price is an observed lagged EMA bounded by the frozen entry anchor. Order spacing, recovery retracement, fair-value close, an adverse limit, failed recovery and three-hour expiry are separate conditions. A reduction may occur below the average entry. A 15-minute cooldown follows a booked close.
- Initial six-month window is [2026-05-25, 2026-11-25) UTC for all 50 symbols. Promising parameter sets are eligible for unchanged full history. There is no statistical prescreen or mandatory baseline. Net ROI, daily Sharpe, execution constraints, P&L concentration and the ratio of fills to completed lifecycles are evaluated together.
- A two-day [2026-08-14, 2026-08-16) diagnostic was scheduled independently of the six-month outcome to inspect orders and lifecycle paths. Diagnostic runs start with fresh inventory and have their own exact audit replays. Their figures describe those windows. They should not be extrapolated to the full six months.
- The strategy uses aggregate aggressive trade volume as a measure of pressure. It cannot distinguish liquidation mandates or observe resting book depth. V2 fills at scheduled next-open prices with fixed friction and no capacity constraint. The selected Liquid 50 was formed using November 2026 activity. The six-month and two-year windows overlap.

Raw submissions and complete evidence are stored under data/research/shock-inventory. Six-month jobs run in the existing V2 registry on port 8791. Short diagnostic jobs run on port 8765 alongside the legacy artifacts, using the same scheduler and audited data.
